The bracket move — up the wall and over

Zones A, B and C all carry 2–3′ soffit overhangs — field walls, gable rakes and dormers alike. The overhang forces the frame line outside the drip line: frames set ~3′-6″ to 4′ off the wall face, side brackets carry the lower decks back to the wall for girt, Hardie and wainscot work, and the top deck rides the frame line under the soffit for the EIFS overhead. One standing setup, every work plane, no re-set between passes.

Corner & return details — plan view

1 · Outside corner

Run 1 carries through one bay past the corner; Run 2 butts to it. Bracket decks on both legs hold ≤14″ to each face and wrap the corner. The notch between runs closes with lapped, cleated infill planks at every deck level. Guardrail is continuous around the outside.

2 · Inside corner (courtyards)

The bracket decks are what turn the inside corner — they run tight to both faces and meet at the corner. The frame runs themselves stop clear of each other; the pocket between them at frame-line depth gets bracket-supported infill. On 916 LF of courtyard wall this detail repeats at 16 corners.

3 · 2′-8″ return — run does not jog

Never jog a buck run 2′-8″. Hold the frame line straight off the proud drip line. At the proud face, brackets hold ≤14″. At the recess the bracket deck sits ~3′-8″ off the wall — that segment gets an inside guardrail, and the 2′-8″ return face plus the recessed run are worked from the notch deck at arm's reach. The resident buildings repeat this at every projecting bay — dozens of returns per building.

Standing requirements

  • Frame line outboard of the drip line at zones A/B/C/D/E/G; side brackets at lower decks; top deck at frame line under the soffit. Brackets go on with the frames — not retrofitted after decking.
  • Corners per Details 1–3 — run-through at outside corners, bracket-turn at inside corners, straight line past 2′-8″ returns with inside rail at recesses.
  • 4:1 height to least base dimension. Outriggers before ties everywhere except Zone F.
  • Guardrails and toeboards at every deck over 10′ — bracket decks included.
  • Screw jacks and mudsills throughout — grade moves on nearly every elevation.
  • Ties penetrate the fluid-applied AV barrier and 3″ polyiso; every one avoided is a detail you don't seal and warrant.
  • High-impact mesh on courtyard columns; courtyard slab is a finish surface — protection and mud control.

Open items

  • Zone splits — assign dormer count and dock/pool runs from elevations to firm the amber quantities.
  • Structural — confirm the ≈28′ main gable peak; get the Zone F engineered erection drawing.
  • Site logistics — crane windows before connectors close; 916 LF of courtyard wall rides on it.
